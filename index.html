A European tradition
free for everyone
just as it should be

Wander down lanes of sparkling lights and wooden chalets filled with artisan gifts, steaming European foods and festive spiced drinks. Enjoy live holiday music, the city’s 30ft Christmas tree, a skating rink, and more... There's holiday magic all along the waterfront!

Visit us day or night, once or again. In the spirit of season, the Shipyards Christmas market is absolutely free for everyone to attend – exactly how it is in Europe.

Outdoor rink ✦ Free to skate

Skate at
the market

Lace up those skates! The Shipyards Christmas Market is not organized by the City of North Vancouver, but their skating rink is a magical addition to our market. Skating entry is free: no booking, no session times, just get on when there's room!
